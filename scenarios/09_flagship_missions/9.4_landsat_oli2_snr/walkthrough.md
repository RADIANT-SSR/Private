# 9.4 Landsat 9 OLI-2 — Multi-Configuration SNR at Typical Radiance, Per-Element Coated Optics

**Mission**: Landsat 9 OLI-2, 705 km SSO, 135 mm four-mirror off-axis anastigmat pushbroom
imager, 9 bands (VNIR Si PIN + SWIR HgCdTe on one 210 K FPA), 14-bit.
**Claims validated**: (1) RADIANT's aperture-to-electrons radiometry predicts the published
per-band SNR at L_typ; (2) a full **per-element prescription with synthetic coatings**
(first scenario to exercise Mode 5 end-to-end) reproduces the same result as a lumped
throughput, while exposing every optic's R/T/ε model individually; (3) all eight 30 m
bands run as **one ADR-0010 configuration set**.

## Design

Each band drives the chain with a flat user-radiance spectrum equal to the published
L_typ [W/m²/sr/µm] through a vacuum path (`atmosphere.model: exo`) — the requirement and
the on-orbit measurement are both stated at-aperture. Extended scene (EE_box = 1, the
regime OLI's per-detector uniform-scene SNR is defined in), no TDI, 3.6 ms integration
(30 m bands) / 1.8 ms (pan).

The optical train is **not** a `transmission_scalar`: it is six `optical_elements`
entries — M1–M4 (protected-silver reflectance curve, ε = 1−R Kirchhoff-derived per
Rule 5), an AR-coated FPA window, and the band's interference filter. **Every curve is
synthetic** — no per-surface curves are published for OLI — generated by
`scripts/gen_oli2_coatings.py` from vendor-typical anchors, with the filter 50% points
pinned to the published band edges. Provenance and recall-confidence classes for every
number: `docs/validation/landsat_oli2_source_data.md`.

**The multi-configuration study** (`oli2_30m_bands_study.yaml`): the eight 30 m bands are
eight configurations of one shared instrument; only radiance file, band edges, QE, and
dark rate are configured (5 dot-paths). ADR-0010 D-7 shares the element document across
configurations, so the per-band filter is expressed as the **composite butcher-block
element** (`data/filter_butcher_block.csv`, pointwise union of the eight non-overlapping
strips — physically what sits over the FPA); each configuration's band edges select its
strip. The pan band (B8) overlaps green/red spectrally and cannot join the union — it
ships standalone (`oli2_b08_snr_ltyp.yaml`; this is Gap 103's recorded re-audit
instance). Even without the overlap, 9 bands would exceed the 8-configuration format cap.

## Run

From this folder:

```bash
radiant validate oli2_30m_bands_study.yaml          # all 8 configurations
radiant run oli2_30m_bands_study.yaml --configuration B4_Red
radiant run oli2_b08_snr_ltyp.yaml                  # pan, standalone
radiant run oli2_b04_snr_ltyp.yaml                  # any band standalone (own filter element)
```

or open `oli2_30m_bands_study.yaml` in the GUI (configuration tab strip; see
`gui_workflow.md` for where to see each optic's coating model).

## Expected results (chain central values vs published)

| Band | RADIANT SNR [-] | Required [-] [Irons 2012] | L8 measured [-] (recalled ±15%) [Morfitt 2015] | Prediction / measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 CA | 177 | ≥130 | ~230 | 0.77 |
| B2 Blue | 496 | ≥130 | ~360 | 1.38 |
| B3 Green | 472 | ≥100 | ~300 | 1.57 |
| B4 Red | 341 | ≥90 | ~225 | 1.51 |
| B5 NIR | 238 | ≥90 | ~200 | 1.19 |
| B6 SWIR1 | 328 | ≥100 | ~265 | 1.24 |
| B7 SWIR2 | 367 | ≥100 | ~180 | 2.04 |
| B8 Pan | 227 | ≥80 | ~145 | 1.56 |
| B9 Cirrus | 139 | ≥50 | ~165 | 0.84 |

Signals run 39.5 ke- (B9) to 282 ke- (B2); shot-noise-dominated in every band (read
noise 200 e- RMS is ≤6% of total noise; dark ≤180 e-/frame even for SWIR).

**Verdict: CONSISTENT.** Every prediction clears the requirement; the ratio to flight
sits in the 0.8–2.0× envelope the 9.1 dossier established for assumption-class QE·τ.
Central predictions land *above* flight where band-average QE and filter peak are
generous, because RADIANT models no PRNU, striping, or calibration-transfer noise.
B1 and B9 land *below* the recalled flight values: both are narrow bands (16 nm /
21 nm) where the band-edge roll-off of the synthetic super-Gaussian filter eats a
larger fraction of the integral and the assumed QE (0.70 at 443 nm; 0.80 for 1.37 µm
MCT) is deliberately conservative — the flight instrument evidently does better. The
recalled measured values themselves carry ±15% (see gaps.md: verification pass).

**Study vs standalone equivalence**: B3–B7, B9 identical to 2 decimal places; B1 +0.4%,
B2 +0.5%. Cause: the B1/B2 published edges are 1 nm apart (0.451/0.452 µm), so the
composite union carries both strips' roll-off tails across the seam, while the
standalone files carry one filter each. The real assembly separates the strips
*spatially* (different detector rows), so the standalone value is the faithful one at
the seam; 0.5% is far inside the coating-envelope uncertainty (±0.06 compound).

## Physics notes (read before editing)

- **Regime**: extended in every configuration — EE_box is not applied (Rule 9), and the
  (ε, T) target doors are unused: the source term is the user-radiance table alone.
- **Thermal self-emission of the train is modeled and non-binding**: elements carry
  Kirchhoff ε at 293 K (mirrors) / 240 K (window) / 210 K (filter); at λ ≤ 2.3 µm these
  emit orders of magnitude below L_typ. Deleting the temperatures changes nothing
  visible — they are there because Mode 5 models them, not because they matter here.
- **Unused parameter groups**: platform jitter/smear defaults are untouched (SNR at
  L_typ is a radiometric claim; the spatial path runs at defaults), and
  `gain_e_per_dn`/`full_well_capacity_e` are analysis-mode values — saturation is out
  of scope (see gaps.md).
- **End-to-end throughput** (B4 band-average): 0.975⁴ (four Ag mirrors) × 0.985
  (window) × 0.90 (filter peak) ≈ 0.80 [-]; inspect per-element curves via the GUI
  coating-spectra plot or `plots.coating_spectra()`.

Non-RADIANT inputs: everything under `data/` (21 CSVs) — regenerated byte-identically by
`python scripts/gen_oli2_coatings.py` (Rule 26 manifest in that script's docstring).

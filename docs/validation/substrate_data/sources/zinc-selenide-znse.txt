Zinc Selenide ZnSe
Zinc Selenide (ZnSe) SDS
Download
Zinc Selenide (ZnSe) Data Sheet
Download
ZnSe is used widely for IR components, windows and lenses, and for spectroscopic ATR prisms. Zinc Selenide is one of the materials of choice for CO2 laser optics operating at 10.6 microns.
Zinc Selenide is produced by synthesis from Zinc vapour and H2Se gas, forming as sheets on Graphite susceptors. Zinc Selenide is microcrystalline in structure, the grain size being controlled to produce maximum strength. Single crystal ZnSe is available, and has been reported as having lower absorption and thus more effective for CO2 optics, although we have no source and cannot currently offer single crystal ZnSe.
Zinc Selenide oxidizes significantly at 300°C, exhibits plastic deformation at about 500°C and dissociates about 700°C. For safety, Zinc Selenide windows should not be used above 250°C in normal atmosphere
